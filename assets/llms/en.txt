# Del Piccolo Diavolo — Staffordshire Bull Terrier Kennel

> Ethical Staffordshire Bull Terrier kennel, active since 2013 in Ostellato (Ferrara, Emilia-Romagna, Italy). Responsible selection focused on health, breed type and temperament. Puppies released with ENCI pedigree, genetic testing of the breeding stock (L2HGA and HC, HSF4 gene; degenerative myelopathy, SOD1 gene, on the active breeding females), microchip, vaccinations and lifelong support for the family.

Useful information about this site:

- Breeder: Paolo Boldrini
- Location: Ostellato (FE), Emilia-Romagna, Italy
- Breed: Staffordshire Bull Terrier
- Bloodlines: English (Elitebull) and Irish (Lackyle)
- Results: Italian and foreign Champions, 4th place at the World Dog Show
