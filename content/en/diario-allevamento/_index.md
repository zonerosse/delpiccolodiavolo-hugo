This is where we record what actually happens at the kennel, litter by litter: who the parents are and why we chose them, how many puppies were born, how they grow through the first weeks, and when they are ready to go home.

It is not a listings page. It is how we show the way we work to people who are considering a puppy and do not know us yet: the pairings we plan months ahead, the genetic tests carried out before every mating, and the litters that are fully reserved before they are even born.

Reading through it tells you two things. First, that we have **few litters a year**, not one a month. Second, that every birth has a story behind it — choosing the stud, checking the lines, the waiting — that is worth telling in full.
