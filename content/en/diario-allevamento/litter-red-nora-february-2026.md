## Why this litter is special

### The sire: Vangerbull line

Red carries the **Vangerbull** prefix, one of the most respected English lines known for physical presence and powerful structure. Solid build, typical head, correct movement.

### The dam: already mother of Champions

Nora is not just any breeding female. She is already the **mother of two Italian Champions**:

- **Bilquis Goddess Diabhal** — Italian Beauty Champion ENCI, 4th place at the World Dog Show Zagreb 2024
- **Lothar Matthaus** — Young Italian Champion ENCI + Slovenian Champion 🇸🇮, currently showing with over 20 exhibitions

This proves her ability to pass quality to her offspring.

## Genetic testing

Both parents are tested **Clear** for the main genetic diseases of the breed:

| Test | Red | Nora |
|------|-----|------|
| L2HGA (L-2-hydroxyglutaric aciduria) | N/N Clear | N/N Clear |
| HC (Hereditary Cataract) | N/N Clear | N/N Clear |
| Dentition | Complete scissor bite | Complete scissor bite |

Puppies will be born **genetically healthy** for L2HGA and HC (all Clear or Carrier, no Affected possible).
